## Notes

1. Fetch environment: WebFetch honors robots.txt and TLS; sos.alabama.gov (robots/TLS failure), sos.ks.gov statistics page (robots), mielections.us (timeout), apps.arizona.vote (403), mvic.sos.state.mi.us (403), and several elections.il.gov pages (403) could not be read. Curl from this container is denied at the egress proxy for every state host, so a browser or a different runner is needed for those.
2. Not-yet-held primaries as of Aug 15: AK (Aug 18), FL (Aug 18), DE (Sep 15), MA (Sep 1). For these the filed candidate list is the best official source and is linked in column (b).
3. Held but not yet certified as of Aug 15: CT (Aug 11), KS (Aug 4), MI (Aug 4), MN (Aug 11), MO (Aug 4). Their linked results are unofficial; certification dates fall between Aug 24 and Aug 26 in most of these states.
4. Certified primary results were confirmed readable today for CA (PDF/XLSX), HI (PDF), ID (PDF), IA (PDF), KY (PDF), MD (HTML), MS (PDF). AZ and CO canvass PDFs exist at the URLs given but the fetch tool extracted no text; treat them as image or complex layout PDFs and open in a PDF reader.
5. Party label conventions to normalize: three-letter codes (CA, IA canvass, ID, FL), single letters in headers (HI, KS), full names (CO, DE, IA general list, MD, MI, MS, MO), registration rather than nomination (AK), DFL instead of Democratic (MN), party carried in file or page title only (ME, MA, KY certification sections).
6. Louisiana switched to closed party primaries for Congress in 2026 (Apr 18 first primary, May 30 second); do not expect the old jungle-primary structure.
