The short version: M2V is a free, nonpartisan, independent voter guide. It has no accounts and collects no personal data. Match percentages are information, not advice or endorsement. Your election office is the final authority on ballots, deadlines, and rules. The same text appears inside the app under Terms of use.

1. What M2V is

Match2Vote (M2V) is a free, nonpartisan voter guide. It shows candidates in covered November 2026 races, the positions they have publicly taken, with a source for each, and how those positions line up with your own answers to a short quiz. It is an information tool. It is not a ballot, a voter-registration service, or an official election resource.

2. No accounts, no personal data

M2V has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts. Your quiz answers, the issues you mark as mattering most, the candidates you mark on your sample ballot, and your display preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. The privacy policy describes the two network requests the app can make.

3. Independence

M2V is developed and published by Kiki Taylor, an independent developer based in Connecticut. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, funded by, or acting for any government agency, election office, political party, campaign, or political committee. It favors no party and no candidate. Party labels appear for identification only and play no role in how matches are scored.

4. Information, not advice

Match percentages are a comparison between your quiz answers and positions we have documented from public sources. They are not a recommendation, an endorsement, or a prediction of how a candidate will act in office. Where we have no source for a position, it stays "Not stated" and is left out of the math. You are responsible for your own voting decisions.

5. Accuracy and corrections

We work to keep candidate rosters and positions accurate and current, and every scored position links to its source. Candidates, ballots, and positions change, and errors are possible. Deadlines, rules, and candidate lists shown in the app are summaries; your state or local election office is the final authority. If something looks wrong, tell us at match2vote@gmail.com and we will review it against the record.

6. Sample ballot and sharing

The ballot inside M2V is a planning tool for your own use. It is not an official ballot and cannot be submitted anywhere. If you use the share or export features, the image is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.
